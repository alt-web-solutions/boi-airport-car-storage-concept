Early flight information

Parking for the 6am flight?

For early 6am flights, park in the main public carpark and take your keys into the terminal. Fill out the form in front of the Hertz booth and place your keys in the slot.

Staff will bring your car into the storage yard when they arrive.

Long-term storage

Need to leave your vehicle for longer?

Limited long-term parking spaces are available. Contact the team to discuss availability, pricing, and the best option for your vehicle.

Outdoor parking

Vehicles are stored outside. You may wish to provide a vehicle cover for longer stays.

Vehicle checks

Staff can start vehicles from time to time and try to advise if an issue is noticed.

COF & registration

Staff may keep an eye on dates, but registration and COF updates remain the owner’s responsibility.

Owner’s risk

Vehicles are parked at the owner’s risk. Covered parking is not currently offered.

Helpful information

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to book a parking space?

No bookings are currently taken. Parking is first in, first served.

What happens if the yard looks full?

If the full sign is up and you are going away for a few days, come in and speak to the team as they may have a plan in place.

Can I arrange long-term parking?

Yes, limited long-term spaces may be available. Contact the team to discuss conditions, availability, and pricing.

Are vehicles insured by the yard?

Vehicles are parked at the owner’s risk. Customers should ensure their own insurance is suitable while travelling.
